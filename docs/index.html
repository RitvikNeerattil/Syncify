Syncify

Your own music, on every computer.

Syncify is a small Windows app for getting songs that aren't on Spotify (unreleased tracks, live versions, rare uploads) into your Spotify Local Files, and keeping them in sync across your computers.

Download for Windows View source on GitHub

Free and open source under the MIT license.

How it works

  1. Search YouTube inside the app and pick the right upload.
  2. Syncify saves the audio as a tagged mp3 (title, artist, album, cover art) in your music folder.
  3. Spotify shows it under Local Files.
  4. Sign in with Google on your other computers and the song shows up there too, synced through a Syncify folder in your own Google Drive.

Your data stays yours

Syncify has no servers. Your songs and song list live on your computers and in your own Google Drive, and Syncify can only see the files it created there. Privacy policy.

Open source

Syncify's full source code is on GitHub under the MIT license, so you can see exactly what it does with your files and your Google account. Bug reports and ideas are welcome in Issues.

Fair use

Syncify is meant for personal listening. Respect the rights of the people who made the music and the terms of the sites you use.
